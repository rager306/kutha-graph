# B3 decision (2026-10-30)

Status: **phase 252 evidence**, preliminary engine comparison; not an ADR and not a production claim.

## Workload

Phase-251 layered `relatedTo`: 3 layers x 256 nodes, fan-out 4, 2,048 edges. Kutha: `hop2.walk.n` over all 256 layer-0 starts (typed IR, in-process). Competitors: one Cypher 2-hop count from all `L0` to `L2` (4,096 paths).

## Measurements

| engine | transport | median | samples |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Kutha walk only | in-process `hop_k` | **161,912 ns** | one pinned run (phase 251 median 170,568 ns) |
| Kutha + lease prepare | in-process `hop_k` | **512,496 ns** | walk 161,912 + `csr_lease.n_ns` 350,584 |
| FalkorDB | raw RESP localhost:6381, graph `b3` | 4,779 µs | 10 runs: 5332/3943/4907/5249/4321/4470/4730/7106/4497/4828 µs |
| Samyama | HTTP `/api/query` localhost:8081, graph `b3s` | 14,511 µs | 10 runs: 20348/14966/11591/14262/19356/13099/33510/9091/14760/13544 µs |

Walk-only ratios: Kutha/Falkor **0.034** (≈29.5× faster), Kutha/Samyama **0.011** (≈89.6× faster). Counting lease prepare: Kutha 512,496 ns, **≈9.3×** faster than Falkor's raw-client median and **≈28.3×** faster than Samyama; against Falkor's own 1.58 ms internal execution it is **≈3.1×**. Falkor's raw-client median is 4.78 ms.

## Decision

**No B3 gap against Kutha** on this workload: B4 (HNSW) and B5 (planner) are **skipped**; do not build indexes without a workload. B6 (Cypher AS OF) still needs M003 and is not thawed by this note.

The phase-251 ratio floor is **not evaluated by this single comparison run**; it is a future Kutha regression gate. This run's own scaling was 2N/N 2.292 (provisional cap 2.28, +0.5% over), 4N/N 3.122 (cap 4.61), 4N/2N 1.362 (cap 2.22). The marginal 2N/N value is one run, not a decision against the floor.

## Caveats

- Transports differ: Kutha in-process, Falkor raw RESP, Samyama HTTP. The gap is large enough to survive client overhead, but this is not a calibrated apples-to-apples production benchmark.
- Output shape differs: Kutha walks per L0 start and returns deduplicated `QueryRows::Nodes`; competitors count 4,096 Cypher paths. Traversal scope is comparable, cardinality is not. This is not evidence for vector search (B4) or planning (B5).
- One same-sitting Kutha run plus the phase-251 pinned median; competitors were measured after a warm-up. No data-load time is included.
- The Falkor graph was loaded earlier; Samyama was loaded via comma `CREATE` plus 32-edge batches. Samyama `UNWIND ... CREATE` is unsupported.
- No temporal AS OF comparison; this is the non-temporal B3 subset. No HNSW/planner/Cypher thaw, no ADR change.
