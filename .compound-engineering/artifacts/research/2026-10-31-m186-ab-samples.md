# M186 A/B raw samples — durable record (2026-10-31)

Provenance: performance logs produced on the host on 2026-10-31 and retained in the gitignored
`tmp/` directory as `ab-base-1..6.log`, `ab-head-1..6.log`, `ab-pre-1..6.log`, `ab-head2-1..6.log`,
`probe-on-1..10.log`, `probe-off-1..10.log`. This file is the durable copy of the numbers; the logs
themselves stay scratch. Absolute times are not comparable across sessions (host load 7.6-9.9 on 12
cores); only interleaved ratios are usable.

The exact `cargo bench` invocation is not recorded in the logs. The toggle binary is identified by the
hash printed in every probe log: `baseline-ad506c2ed323b589` in `tmp/ab-probe-target/release/deps/`.
One binary hash appears across all 20 probe runs, so the toggle was a runtime switch compiled into that
build; the patch that added it and the variable name were **not preserved**, and the binary is gone.
The toggle measurement is therefore recorded evidence, not a reproducible procedure.

Correction note (2026-10-31): four of six `A/B2 head` medians published in
`docs/architecture/benchmark-baseline.md` did not match these logs; they appear
to have been taken from unsorted positional samples (the published `ingest_plain.n`
value equals the mean of positional samples 3 and 4). The table below recomputes
every median from sorted samples. Consequence: `ingest_keyed.n` is **+12.1%**, not
+9.3%. The AGENTS.md bench gate covers `ingest_plain.n` and `as_of.n` only; those are -4.2% (better) and
+1.7%, so the M186 keep decision stands. `ingest_keyed.n` stays recorded as a secondary signal.
