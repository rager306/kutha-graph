# Claim audit — documentation honesty for v1.76–v1.79 (M183–M186)

Read-only audit of the four most recently closed GSD milestones (v1.76 digest falsifiers and
index, v1.77 admission operator index, v1.78 enrichment admission leases, v1.79 meta lease
overhead A/B). No tracked file was edited, no commit/tag/push, no `graph index`, no cargo, no
`kutha-gov` run. The only file written is this report.

**Verdict:** the *derived percentages* are right and reproducible, and the falsification that
overturned the `as_of.2n` regression is present in the benchmark note, the phase summary, the
phase verification, and the CHANGELOG process entry. Four things are not: (1) the harness
`STATE.md` and the `.kutha/ROADMAP.md` still send the next agent after an "open residual" that
the toggle experiment killed; (2) four of the six A/B 2 head medians in the committed benchmark
table cannot be reproduced from the surviving raw logs; (3) the falsification's "env-gated
`log_ids`" toggle does not exist anywhere in the repository or its history; (4) two close
entries carry test counts that contradict the code and the same entry's own numbers.

Highest-value fix, one line: replace the `as_of.2n` paragraph in `.kutha/STATE.md` (§ Next
action) with the toggle result and the real `ingest_plain.n` +11.4% cost.
